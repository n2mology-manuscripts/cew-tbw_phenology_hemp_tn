# Figures and supporting tables

Figure 1. Seasonal abundance, species composition and variety associations of Chloridea virescens and Helicoverpa zea in Tennessee hemp. Panels are grouped by research location and collection year. Left: total larvae divided by inspected plants on each collection date, on a common 0-15 scale. Day labels are grouped by month; calendar spacing is retained within each panel, and sampling windows differ. Smooth lines interpolate the observed points for visual guidance without estimating an inferential seasonal model. Colored strips show seasonal percentages of both species; header counts give total larvae. Black whiskers show marginal 95% whole-row bootstrap intervals for the C. virescens share at the boundary between species; the H. zea interval is its complement. Right: filled symbols show pooled seasonal larvae per observed plant visit, on a common 0-25 scale; open symbols show individual row rates. Horizontal lines show marginal 95% percentile intervals from 4,999 whole-row resamples. Four or five rows contributed per variety, retaining plant subsamples and repeated visits. CI n/a marks the all-zero H. zea response for Lifter at ETREC-PS in 2023, for which no bootstrap upper bound was estimable. Intervals are exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Full effort, counts and estimates appear in Supplementary Tables S1-S4. The separate NETREC 2022 nitrogen experiment is shown in Figure 2.

Figure 2. Seasonal abundance, species composition, variety associations and nitrogen contrasts in the separate NETREC 2022 experiment. Left: total larvae divided by inspected plants on each collection date, on the same 0-15 scale as Figure 1. Smooth lines interpolate observed points for visual guidance; inspected-plant counts varied from 32 to 60 per date (Supplementary Table S2). The composition strip summarizes 1,134 larvae; its black whisker shows the marginal 95% whole-block bootstrap interval for the C. virescens share, with complementary bounds for H. zea. Middle: pooled seasonal larvae per observed plant visit for Cherry Wine and Sweetened, on the same 0-25 scale as Figure 1. Filled symbols show pooled rates; open symbols show the three block rates. Horizontal lines show marginal 95% percentile intervals from 4,999 whole-block resamples. Rates pool all available nitrogen treatments without nitrogen adjustment; plot 310 remains unavailable. Only three blocks contribute, and intervals are exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Right: mean seasonal rate differences (larvae per observed plant visit) from zero N and 95% t intervals. Open symbols show individual within-block differences; filled symbols show their means. Nitrogen was assigned to whole plots, each containing two variety subplots. Within each complete whole plot, the two subplot rates were weighted equally. Labels give lb N/acre and complete-block counts in parentheses. Plot 310 was unavailable, leaving two blocks at 150 N and three at other rates; t intervals therefore have one or two degrees of freedom. Curves and contrasts describe the observed sampling schedule. Broad intervals spanning zero do not establish equivalence. The allocation and numerical contrasts are provided in Supplementary Tables S5-S7.
