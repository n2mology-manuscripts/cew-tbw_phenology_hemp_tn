# Figures and supporting tables

Figure 1. Seasonal abundance, species composition and variety associations of *Chloridea virescens* and *Helicoverpa zea* in Tennessee hemp. Location-year groups are ETREC-PS in 2022 (A) and 2023 (B), HRREC in 2022 (C) and 2023 (D), and NETREC in 2023 (E). Left: larvae per inspected plant by collection date; smooth lines interpolate observations for visual guidance. Composition strips show species percentages and total counts, with black whiskers indicating uncertainty in the *C. virescens* share. Right: filled symbols show pooled larvae per observed plant visit, open symbols show row rates, and horizontal lines indicate uncertainty. Intervals are marginal 95% whole-row bootstrap intervals based on four or five rows, exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons. “CI n/a” denotes an all-zero response with no reported interval. Summaries describe the recorded sampling periods. Supporting data appear in Supplementary Tables S1–S4.

Figure 2. Seasonal abundance, species composition, variety associations and nitrogen contrasts for *Chloridea virescens* and *Helicoverpa zea* at NETREC in 2022. A: larvae per inspected plant by collection date; smooth lines interpolate observations. The composition strip summarizes 1,134 larvae, with its black whisker indicating uncertainty in the *C. virescens* share. B: filled symbols show pooled larvae per observed plant visit for each variety; open symbols show block rates. Composition and variety intervals are marginal 95% whole-block bootstrap intervals based on three blocks. Variety rates pool available nitrogen treatments without nitrogen adjustment. C: filled symbols show mean within-block rate differences from zero N, open symbols show block differences, and horizontal lines show 95% t intervals. Whole-plot rates weight the two variety subplots equally. Labels identify nitrogen rates (lb N/acre) and complete-block counts: two at 150 N and three at other rates. Summaries describe the recorded sampling period; bootstrap intervals are exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons. Supporting data appear in Supplementary Tables S2 and S5–S7.
